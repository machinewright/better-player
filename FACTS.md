   from 1975. 16 March 2022: all four majors use a 10-point tiebreak at 6-6 in the final set (from RG 2022). Before:
   AO 10-pt at 6-6, Wimbledon 7-pt at 12-12, USO 7-pt at 6-6, RG none. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_scoring_system,
   https://wtop.com/sports/2022/03/all-grand-slams-to-use-10-point-tiebreaker-in-final-set/
